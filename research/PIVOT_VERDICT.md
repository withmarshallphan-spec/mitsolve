# Kill test verdict (3 Nov 2026)

This supersedes the RefillAhead recommendation in `RESEARCH.md`.

**Context:** solo applicant, gap-year high-school graduate in Batam, Indonesia. Reach is through WhatsApp. The deadline is 6 Nov 2026, 12:00 pm ET (Solve's challenge pages); some third-party sites say 2 Nov, so **verify in the portal**.

## The five tests

1. It removes the actual blocker that causes the harm.
2. It creates a new capability, not a feature of an existing system.
3. The outcome is visible and measurable.
4. The applicant can put a working prototype in front of real users by 5 Nov, with no institutional approval.
5. It hasn't already been done by a past Solver or a dominant incumbent.

## Killed

### RefillAhead (forecast-triggered chronic-care refills)
- **Fails test 1:** patients drop out of care mainly because staff, medicine and records disappear, not because nobody knew whom to refill.
- **Fails test 2:** HIV record systems already produce "due for refill" lists.
- **Fails test 4:** it needs clinics, data access and approvals.
- **False premise:** the 2025 aid cuts took effect immediately, so there was no warning to act on.

### Job-offer verification bot for Batam factory jobs
- **Test 1, mostly fails.** The largest losses are payments victims make *knowingly*, to brokers selling insider access ("orang dalam"): Rp3–6 juta in Batam (FSPMI) and Rp3–33 juta in Java and Banten cases. Victims know these people aren't official HR. A bot only addresses the impersonation, phishing and APK share: frequent, but around Rp1 juta per victim.
- **Test 2, weak.**
  - Kemnaker is building the same verification: mandatory vacancy reporting since 2026, 81k vacancies on Karirhub, and a planned QR code per vacancy.
  - KP2MI's SISKOP2MI already checks overseas agencies.
  - Mafindo's CekSumber covers general scam checks on WhatsApp.
- **Corrections to earlier claims.**
  - The Sumitomo-impersonation case was **one ring, 140 victims, Rp140 juta total**, not "hundreds of victims".
  - The "15,189 seekers vs 5,242 placed" and "26 closures" figures could not be independently confirmed. Don't cite them without the primary source.
  - Fees are illegal for factory-operator roles, not for *every* role (Kepmen 230/2003).

## Survivor: parent-held reading and maths check on WhatsApp, with practice matched to the child's level

**Challenge:** 2027 Global Learning Challenge. It asks for better assessment of what learners can actually do, and for **learning gains, not engagement**.

**Problem (evidence)**
- More than 3 in 4 Indonesian 15-year-olds are below minimum proficiency in reading and maths (OECD, PISA 2022).
- About half of grade 3 pupils are below minimum literacy, and two in three are below minimum numeracy (INOVASI, 18,370 pupils).
- Parents get no individual result before grade 6. The national assessment (Asesmen Nasional) samples pupils and gives no individual scores. The new grade 6 test (TKA) reports only through school operators.

**Blocker:** nobody tracks the child's actual level, so practice at home and in class isn't matched to it.
- Teachers often follow the textbook without tracking each child's progress (Pemantik toolkit authors).
- Parents misjudge their child's level. In Malawi, parents' beliefs were off by 20 points on average, and correcting them changed what parents invested (Dizon-Ross).

**Why it should work (causal evidence)**
- Teaching at the child's level (TaRL) is among the most cost-effective education interventions.
- Phone-based teaching at the child's level (ConnectEd, Youth Impact) raised numeracy by 0.12 SD in Botswana, with replications across five countries.
- Engaging parents over WhatsApp improved how accurately they picked the next learning step (India).

**What's new**
- No Indonesian tool was found that gives *parents* an objective level for their own child through WhatsApp voice notes, followed by matched practice and a re-check.
- Neighbours, not the same thing:
  - Pemantik: an assessment toolkit for teachers.
  - The ministry, Tanoto, Gates and UNICEF are pushing real-time diagnostic assessment for teachers (Apr 2026).
  - Bookbot: a reading app for children, reading only.
  - Rocket Learning: India only, early childhood.

**Can it be tested by 5 Nov (test 4)? Yes. This is its main advantage.**
- Reach 10–20 children in grades 1–4 through family WhatsApp groups.
- Before showing any result, ask the parent what level they think the child is at.
- The child reads a card in a voice note and answers maths items.
- Score the child, and send back the level plus 7 days of practice.
- The **belief gap** (share of parents who overestimated their child's level) is real evidence available within 48 hours. The re-check comes after 4 weeks.

**Risks**
- Seen as an "Indonesian Rocket Learning". Mitigation: lead with assessment and the measured belief gap.
- Bookbot overlap. Mitigation: no app to install, WhatsApp voice notes, maths included, results go to the parent.
- Assessment alone teaches nothing. Mitigation: the practice loop and the re-check are core.
- Scoring by hand doesn't scale. Mitigation: a person scores during the prototype; Indonesian speech-to-text comes later.
- Children's voice data. Mitigation: parental consent, and audio deleted after scoring.
